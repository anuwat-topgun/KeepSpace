KeepSpace Support

Need help with a scan, cleanup, or cloud backup? Tell us what happened and we’ll help you find the next step.

01

Check the basics

Confirm that KeepSpace has access to the photos you selected, that your device has free space, and that your network meets your Wi-Fi-only setting.

02

Cloud backup help

If a provider connection expires, disconnect and reconnect it from Settings → Cloud, then retry the failed item.

03

Contact support

Email us with the app version, device model, operating-system version, and a short description of the issue.

Email support@itston.com Never include private photos, OAuth tokens, passwords, recognized text, or precise location information in your message.

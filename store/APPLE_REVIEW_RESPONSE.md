## 2. Purpose, audience, problem, and value

KeepSpace is a photo-library organization and storage-cleanup utility for iPhone and iPad owners. It helps people understand what occupies storage, review similar or blurry photos, screenshots, large videos, and screen recordings, keep their preferred items, and ask iOS to remove only the items they explicitly select. Optional direct cloud backup lets the user copy selected files to their own Google Drive or personal OneDrive account. The value is a guided, privacy-focused workflow: analysis stays on the device, deletions remain user controlled, and KeepSpace does not operate a content backend.

## 3. Setup and access instructions

KeepSpace has no KeepSpace account, password, invitation, or organization login. A reviewer can use the app immediately after granting full or limited photo access. For useful results, the device library should contain several photos (including visually similar photos and screenshots) and at least one video.

Suggested path: launch > complete onboarding > grant Photos access > Home > Clean > Library > Settings > Cloud > Settings > Subscription.

Cloud Backup is optional. The reviewer may sign in with any Google account or any personal Microsoft account. The Microsoft application intentionally does not accept work or school accounts. No sample file is required; select an item already in the review device's Photos library.

## 4. External services, tools, and platforms

- Apple Photos: local library access, previews, compression output, sharing, and system-controlled deletion confirmation.
- Apple StoreKit / App Store: monthly and yearly subscriptions, lifetime purchase, entitlement status, and restore purchases.
- Google Sign-In and Google Drive API: optional OAuth connection and direct upload of user-selected files to the user's own Drive.
- Microsoft identity platform and Microsoft Graph / OneDrive: optional OAuth connection and direct upload to the user's own personal OneDrive.
- Apple Vision and bundled on-device models: local image/text analysis. The app has no developer-operated backend, advertising SDK, or analytics SDK.

## 5. Regional behavior

Features and app behavior are the same in every supported region. Store prices, currency, tax handling, trial eligibility, and translated system/store text can vary according to the App Store account and storefront.

## 6. Regulated services and third-party rights

KeepSpace does not provide healthcare, financial, gambling, government, VPN, cryptocurrency, or other regulated services. It does not stream, sell, or redistribute third-party media. It only processes the user's own Photos-library items and uploads items the user chooses to accounts the user controls, so no third-party content-rights authorization is required.

## 7. In-app purchase overview and navigation

Open **Settings > Subscription > Upgrade to Pro**. The purchase screen contains:

- Pro Yearly — `com.keepspace.app.pro.annual` — auto-renewing, with a 7-day introductory free trial for eligible new subscribers.
- Pro Monthly — `com.keepspace.app.pro.monthly` — auto-renewing.
- Pro Lifetime — `com.keepspace.app.pro.lifetime` — non-consumable, one-time purchase.

The screen displays StoreKit-provided localized prices, duration, trial and renewal disclosure, Restore, Terms of Use, and Privacy Policy. Pro can also be opened from a feature that exceeds the free allowance. It is not forced at launch or during onboarding.

KeepSpace helps you recover storage without giving up the photos and videos that matter.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
AI analysis runs entirely on your device. Your photos, videos, locations, and recognized text are never sent to KeepSpace.

BACK UP WHERE YOU CHOOSE
• Connect Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive with secure OAuth
• Copy photos, screenshots, or receipts directly from your device to your account
• Choose a folder, use Wi-Fi only, and follow upload and verification status
• KeepSpace has no media server and never receives your files
• Version 1.1 always keeps the local original

CLEAN WITH CONFIDENCE
• Find exact duplicates and similar photos
• Compare a group and keep its best shot
• Review blurry photos, old screenshots, screen recordings, and large videos
• See a Safety Score and cleanup plan before anything changes
• Confirm every deletion through Android's system controls

UNDERSTAND YOUR LIBRARY
• Sort screenshots into shopping, receipts, chats, QR codes, tickets, and more
• Protect favorites and meaningful memories by default
• Group trips and events using on-device signals
• See a storage forecast based on how your library grows

SAVE MORE SPACE
Compress large videos and review the saved copy before deciding whether to remove the original.

SMART, NOT SILENT
Nothing is deleted automatically. Items you approve go to Trash, where Android controls the recovery period. Optional Weekly Smart Clean reminders are created locally from your latest scan.

KeepSpace supports phones, tablets, foldables, split screen, and selected-photo access on supported Android versions.

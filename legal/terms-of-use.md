## The service

KeepSpace analyzes media on your device and presents storage estimates, cleanup suggestions, best-shot recommendations, memory groups, receipt details, and optional video compression. These results are automated estimates and may be incomplete or inaccurate.

Cloud Backup is an optional, user-initiated transfer from your device to a Google Drive or Microsoft OneDrive account you connect. KeepSpace does not guarantee provider availability, upload completion, retention, or recovery. Confirm the in-app “Backed up” state and, for important files, verify them in the provider before relying on a cloud copy. Version 1.1 does not delete local originals after upload.

You remain in control of your media. Review every selection and confirm that you have a backup when needed before deleting or replacing a file. KeepSpace asks the operating system to move confirmed items to Recently Deleted or Trash, but recovery periods and availability are controlled by iOS or Android and may vary.

## KeepSpace Free and KeepSpace Pro

KeepSpace is free to download. Scanning your library, seeing every category and its size, the Safety Score, protected memories, Best Shot, the storage forecast, and the confirmation step before any deletion are always free.

On the Free plan, cleanup is limited each calendar month (currently about 1 GB of confirmed deletions), cloud backup is limited each month (currently about 100 files and one connected cloud account), and you can create one storage rule of your own. These limits are shown in **Settings > Subscription** and may change in a future version. KeepSpace Pro removes those limits and adds video compression, browsing screenshot categories, Receipt Filing, and AI Taste.

You can buy KeepSpace Pro as:

- **an auto-renewing subscription**, billed monthly or yearly. The yearly plan may include a free trial if you have not used one before; the trial length, the price, and the billing period are shown on the purchase screen in your local currency before you confirm; or
- **a one-time lifetime purchase**, which does not renew and unlocks Pro for as long as KeepSpace is offered on your store.

Purchases are made through, and paid to, Apple (iOS) or Google Play (Android). Payment is charged to your Apple Account or Google Play account when you confirm. KeepSpace never sees your payment details and has no account system of its own.

**Renewal and cancellation.** A subscription renews automatically for the same period at the price shown unless you cancel before the current period ends. On iOS, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period in your Apple Account settings (**Settings > your name > Subscriptions**). On Android, cancel in **Google Play > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions**. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. If you start a free trial and do not cancel before it ends, it converts to a paid subscription.

**Refunds** are handled by Apple or Google Play under their policies, not by KeepSpace.

**Restoring.** Use **Restore Purchases** on the purchase screen or in **Settings > Subscription** to unlock Pro again on a new device with the same store account. Subscriptions are not shared between an Apple Account and a Google Play account.

**If Pro ends,** KeepSpace never deletes your photos, videos, rules, or cloud files. Free-plan limits apply again to new actions. Rules beyond your first stay saved but are not used, a second connected cloud account stays connected but receives no new uploads, and what AI Taste has already learned is kept.

We may change prices or plans for future purchases. A price change never alters a purchase you have already made, and stores tell you before a subscription renews at a new price when they require it.
